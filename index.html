Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Does Saral Retail handle GST automatically?
Yes. Every bill applies CGST/SGST or IGST automatically based on your GSTIN and the customer's state, and GST-ready reports are generated as you sell — no separate reconciliation step.
Can I run multiple branches on one account?
Yes. Stock, billing and accounting are shared across branches in real time, with branch-wise reports and stock transfers built in from the Growth plan up.
What happens if the internet goes down mid-sale?
You can keep billing at the counter; queued bills sync automatically the moment the connection returns, so nothing needs re-entering.
Which devices and printers are supported?
Saral Retail runs in any modern browser on desktop, tablet or phone, and supports barcode scanners plus laser and thermal receipt printers.
Is my data backed up and secure?
Yes. Your ledger is backed up daily, and staff access is role-based, so cashiers, managers and owners each see only what they need.
Can I switch plans as I add branches?
Yes — plans scale by branch count, and moving up carries your existing ledger, stock and settings with it.
